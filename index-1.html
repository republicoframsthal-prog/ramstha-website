HISTORY OF RAMSTHA

2018

The Beginning

The Republic of Ramstha began as a micronation project. Its earliest national flag was orange with a blue bow-and-arrow design.

21 FEB 2022

Civil War of 21F

The Civil War of 21F became an important turning point in Ramstha's history. Following the conflict, a green-white-orange flag with a blue star was adopted, and the nation was known as Hindu Sthal.

2022–2023

Conflicts & Expansion

Ramstha experienced conflicts involving Republic of Akherica, Redistan and Smar Democratic. According to Ramstha's historical record, territory was added following this period of conflict.

1 JUL 2025

Republic of Ram Sthal

The nation was renamed Republic of Ram Sthal. A new national flag and national anthem were adopted. Ramstha also formed an alliance with Relkania and joined the UMER WhatsApp group.

2026

Modern Ramstha

Ramstha entered a new era with Temple Town as its capital and an Imperial Democracy as its system of government. The nation is now divided into 9 states.
